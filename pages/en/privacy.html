Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. We do not collect your personal data

DeepSeek Harness Desktop Green Portable Launcher is an open-source, green-portable tool that runs on your local machine. It does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (PII).

The only scenarios where the launcher needs internet connectivity are:

  • First-time setup, to download portable Node / Python / dsh runtime binaries.
  • Self-update checks, calling the public Release APIs of GitHub and Gitee.
  • The official dsh runtime sending API requests to your own AI model providers (fully under your control).

None of the above network requests carry your personal information, and none are used for third-party tracking.

2. Publish site and Google AdSense

This project's publish site (dsh-green.website) is hosted on Cloudflare Pages.

If we enable Google AdSense on this site in the future to cover hosting and operational costs, please be aware that:

  • Google AdSense may use cookies on your device to serve personalized advertisements.
  • Google may analyze non-personalized browsing data — such as which pages you visit and how long you stay — to decide which ads to show.
  • Google's data processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • You can clear, disable, or restrict cookies through your browser settings at any time, or use an ad blocker to prevent AdSense ads from loading.

3. GitHub Pages logs

GitHub Pages may automatically log access (IP address, User-Agent, request timestamp) for security and troubleshooting purposes. We cannot directly access or modify those logs. Please refer to the GitHub Pages documentation for details.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not share your data with any advertisers.
  • We do not embed tracking code in the launcher.
  • We do not require you to sign up or submit an email address.
